# Caucasian Eagle: player manual (v0.1)

[← Project index](../README.md)

## Starting

Start the game and choose **Play** in the main menu. You arrive in Moonlit Sanctuary as Nyxara, with six shades around you.

## Moving

| Input | What happens |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Nyxara lifts off and flies upright, low over the ground. Let go and she lands. |
| Shift (hold) | Wing boost: much faster, with a trail of feathers |
| Ctrl (hold) | Walk on foot |
| Space | Jump |
| Space (hold) | Rise into free flight |
| Mouse | Turn the view. Move the mouse down to look from high above, up to look level or slightly upward. |

You can keep moving while attacking. Nyxara keeps facing her target and her legs and wings keep flying.

## Swords

| Input | Attack |
|---|---|
| Left click | **Left Talon**, then **Right Talon**: each click swings the other sword, as long as you keep clicking |
| Right click | **Charged Slash**: slower, wider, hits harder and knocks shades back |
| Q | **Sword Exchange**: Moonfeather and Talonshade swap hands |
| E (hold) | **Sword Barrier**: two spectral swords rise in front of her and block shade orbs, for up to 5 seconds |

If a shade is close but out of reach, a sword attack steps in toward it.

## Skills

Skills fire the moment you press them, even in the middle of a sword attack. Each has a cooldown shown in the skill bar.

| Key | Skill | Cooldown | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| F | **Talon Dash** | 4 s | Dashes straight through the shades in front of her, cutting each one, then throws a lance |
| V | **Feather Cyclone** | 7 s | A cyclone of feathers that she carries with her; it drags nearby shades into her blades |
| C | **Moon Leap** | 9 s | She rises, dives onto her target, and a pillar of moonlight strikes where she lands |

## Combos

Right after a skill, the gold **COMBO** prompt shows two follow-ups. Press left or right click while it is showing.

| After | Left click | Right click |
|---|---|---|
| F · Talon Dash | **Twin Talons**: three lunging slash waves | **Rising Eagle**: an uppercut that launches shades |
| V · Feather Cyclone | **Tempest Triple**: three spinning slashes | **Whirlwind Talons**: throws the cyclone forward; it travels, pulls and shreds |
| C · Moon Leap | **Soul Weave**: volleys of homing feathers from the air | **Moonfall Dive**: a higher leap and a bigger slam |

## The screen

- **Bottom left:** your health (green, red when low), your soul bar (blue), and which sword is in which hand.
- **Bottom centre:** eight slots for your actions with their keys. A darkened slot with a number is cooling down.
- **Above the slots:** the COMBO prompt, with a bar showing how long it stays open.
- **Over each shade:** its health.
- **Top centre:** the name of the move you just used.

## Shades

Shades hover, shoot violet orbs at you and come back about 8 seconds after you destroy them. Block the orbs with the barrier (E) or keep moving.

## The mountain

Move the mouse up to raise the view. Beyond the sanctuary stands the mountain where Amirani is chained, with the eagle at his side and his dog at the chain. You cannot reach it yet.

## Settings

Graphics, audio, controls and interface settings are in the main menu under **Settings**. They are saved for your Windows user and survive reinstalling the game.
