## 0.5.2 · not released · Real trees

**The trees beside the path are real 3D models (2026-11-20).** The owner: "improve trees, create its real 3D models, refine them, their branches and leaves, their roots". Until 0.5.1 every tree of the Ascent was baked into the map: a few crossed pictures on a stick. Now the game draws them itself from a library of models made by `art/trees` (Python): 18 kinds (the Caucasian fir, the great old fir, the young fir, spruce, Scots pine, beech, oak, maple, hornbeam, the golden beech, birch, apple, cherry, orange and pomegranate trees, rhododendron, juniper and dead snags), 58 variants. Each is grown branch by branch: a trunk that flares at its foot into roots that run into the ground, limbs set the way the kind sets them (a fir's whorls, a maple's opposite pairs, the spiral of the others), twigs, and at their ends the kind's own sprays of leaves, boughs of needles or a pine's tufts, from CC0 photographs (ambientCG, Poly Haven; listed in `art/trees/LICENSES.md`). Bark is the photographed bark of each kind, with its relief, at a real bark's brightness. Every model has four levels of detail; far off a tree is a picture of itself rendered in Blender (an impostor atlas). Trees within 150 m cast shadows (a new engine hook: games cast shadows of what they draw). The map now lists its 47,659 near trees (`Maps/EaglesAscent.plants`) instead of baking them; the far forest stays baked, at half its triangles (5.3 M, was 10.4 M), and the compiled map is 758 MB (was 1.3 GB). Trunks still stop Nyxara. `CAUCASIAN_EAGLE_GAME_TREES=0` turns the new trees off. The Map Lab draws the same trees. `tools/nature-test.ps1` runs the nature test unattended (`-NoGameTrees` for the old look). In the lab's 18 review views a frame takes 2.0 to 4.7 ms.

**Every tree is a 3D model; the whole mountain's ground holds her (2026-11-21).** The owner: "in the round path there are a lot big trees they still have very bad assets ... i want all trees to be 3D models, also grape and fruit trees ... also all tree should have collisions - and also whole mountain's grounds or walls should have collisions". The first pass gave the game only the trees within 230 m of the main path; the rest of the forest, which the rings run right through, stayed baked as crude far trees (a stick and a few crossed pictures), and so did the six colossi and the vineyards. Now all 299,664 trees of the map are the game's models; only fallen logs, the gardens' tomatoes and cucumbers, ferns and flowers stay baked (the baked forest is 70,880 triangles, was 5.3 M). New models: the great beech and its golden form (44 to 50 m) and the great spruce (40 m), for the forest's trees over 32 m (a 20 m model scaled to 55 m had leaves 3 m long); the colossus (78 to 84 m, a broad crown on heavy limbs, a trunk flared into eleven roots); and the vine on its trellis (two posts and two wires, a gnarled stem, two arms along the lower wire, this year's shoots up and over with grape leaves, bunches of grapes built berry by berry). 23 models, 71 variants; 95 far pictures. Every tree holds her: `.trees` now has shrubs and junipers (by their stems) and each vine's two posts and stem, 300,996 trunks in all. Ground: the beach cells with a corner in the sea had no collision (with all four corners asked to be above the sea); now any cell above the sea is ground, near land and outer land. The far land beyond the outer land is walkable collision where it is above the sea (none of it is, as built: the outer land covers it). New test, the ground probe (`CAUCASIAN_EAGLE_DEMO=6`, `ground.tsv`): every 16 m over the near and the outer land, above the sea, the engine's collision is asked for ground within 8 m of the land drawn there; land steeper than ~58 degrees is a wall (the engine stands no one on a face over 60 degrees; there `EaglesAscent::KeepOutOfRock` holds her, from the same heights). Before the fix 228 holes along the shore; after it `GROUND_PASS`: 82,345 columns over land, 51,981 ground, 30,296 walls, 68 in caves, no hole. Nature test `NATURE_PASS` (24 trunks reached, none passed through), jars' test `WAYS_PASS`, the creatures' tour `CREATURES_PASS`. In the lab's 18 review views and 12 views along the rings a frame takes 1.6 to 6.5 ms.

**3D until a tree is a few pixels; the whole mountain's forest from anywhere; snow firs; beech bark (2026-11-21).** Two things were left of "i dont need trees which are not 3D". A tree became its picture once it was about 190 m away (a 20 m tree then still ~100 px tall), and the game drew no tree farther than 3.2 km: from the sea or from the far side the mountain stood bare, its forest having been baked before. Now every tree is a 3D model (levels 0 to 3; level 3 is the trunk, the main limbs and a few clusters, 108 to 520 triangles) until it is about 25 px tall (`AscentTrees::Picture`); only beyond that is it its picture. Pictures are merged into one mesh per group: a square's trees in two groups by size (a great tree stays 3D out to about 1.9 km, a shrub to about 0.5 km), and a block of 4 x 4 squares (768 m) merged whole once all its trees are pictures. Groups are merged nearest first, 12,000 trees a frame, and a merged mesh not drawn for 30 s is let go. The whole forest is drawn from anywhere: the views from the sea and from the side show the mountain wooded again. Snow firs: the snow lay on a third of every spray, so from any distance a snow fir read as frosted pale blue-white (a mip of the leaves averages in linear light, where white outweighs dark needles six to one); now about a seventh, in firmer lumps, a little less than white, and they read as dark green firs carrying snow. Beech bark: the beeches, golden beeches, great beeches and colossi had a nearly flat pale grey bark (ambientCG Bark011), and the great trunks along the rings stood like concrete columns. Now it is ambientCG Bark010 (smooth grey), mottled with paler lichen and darker green-grey patches of damp, with the dashes of its lenticels (`textures.py`: `beech_bark`). Engine: instance data goes into its buffer as a ring (WRITE_NO_OVERWRITE after the last batch, WRITE_DISCARD only when it wraps). A DISCARD for every draw had renamed the whole buffer (up to 2.3 MB) some 300 times a frame, and the driver waited for the GPU inside Map: 14 ms of the trees' time in 6 of 18 views. Also new: GPU stopwatches for games (`Renderer::BeginGameGpuStopwatch`). The lab's review shots write the GPU's own time for the frame and for the trees, because with the screen asleep the frames are paced to 60 Hz whatever vsync says. In the 18 review views and 12 ring views the trees take 0.1 to 2.2 ms of the GPU and 0.2 to 4.0 ms of the CPU, at most 412 draws (measured while another program kept most of the CPU busy). On the staged game: nature test `NATURE_PASS` (24 trunks reached, none passed through), jars' test `WAYS_PASS`, the creatures' tour `CREATURES_PASS`.

**The ground's plants are 3D models too: fallen logs, tomatoes, cucumbers, ferns, flowers (2026-11-21).** The owner: "Fallen logs, tomatoes, cucumbers, ferns and flowers are still built into the map - i want also them to be 3D". They were the last of the baked forest (10,566 plants, 70,880 triangles): each tomato, cucumber, fern and clump of flowers two or three crossed pictures, each log a six-sided tube. Now the game draws them from five new models of the tree library (`art/trees/plants.py`: 17 variants, each with four levels of detail, all 3D; their pictures made by `art/trees/plant_textures.py` from CC0 photographs by ambientCG, listed in `art/trees/LICENSES.md`, and painted ones). The fallen log (a 12 m model scaled to each log's length): bark on an uneven round, broken limbs, moss cushions on its top, bracket fungi on its sides, grass where it meets the earth; thrown by the wind with its root plate torn up with it (earth held in its roots), snapped with both ends splintered and rotten, or felled and sawn. The tomato on its hazel stake, tied to it: a zigzag stem, compound leaves (the lowest yellowing), side shoots, trusses of tomatoes ripe low down and green higher up, its flowers at the top. The cucumber: vines running out over the ground, lobed leaves held up on their stalks in a mound, coiled tendrils, yellow flowers, cucumbers lying among the leaves. The fern: a shuttlecock of fronds arching out of a scaly crown, fiddleheads uncurling in its middle. Meadow flowers in their grass: oxeye daisies, poppies, bellflowers and buttercups, in drifts of one kind (poppies below 600 m, bellflowers above). Nothing of the forest is baked now. Each plant's foot follows the ground's slope (the map gives the ground's rise under it, and its stems stay upright), so a cucumber's vines or a fern's fronds neither float nor sink into a slope. A log lies along the straightest line of ground through its middle that is clear of every trunk, the path, the map's places and rock, tilted as the ground runs along it and settled into the earth; where its whole length will not fit it is shorter. 92 logs (18 shortened, 14 left out where no line would hold one); the 31 ferns and flowers a log would lie on are left out. The logs and the tomatoes' stakes hold Nyxara: a log is a chain of short pieces in `.trees` (306,069 trunks in all, was 300,996), and a trunk under 2 m holds her only up to just over its top, so she can jump over a log or a stake. The plants have no far picture: each is its 3D model until it is a few pixels tall, and then it is not drawn; a group of plants only is never merged into a far mesh; the small ones cast shadows within 60 m. In the Map Lab's 18 review views and 12 ring views a frame takes 1.8 to 5.6 ms; the trees and plants take 0.1 to 1.9 ms of the GPU and 0.1 to 2.6 ms of the CPU, at most 440 draws (the views from before the plants: 0.1 to 2.2 ms of the GPU, 0.2 to 4.0 ms of the CPU, at most 412 draws, measured while another program loaded the CPU). On the staged game: nature test `NATURE_PASS` (24 trunks reached, none passed through), jars' test `WAYS_PASS`, the creatures' tour `CREATURES_PASS`, the ground probe `GROUND_PASS` (no hole).

## 0.5.1 · 2026-11-20 · The Ascent's menu; why Windows warns

**Esc opens the game's menu in the Ascent (2026-11-20).** The owner: "from game the Esc give you the testing lab options". In 0.5.0 Esc on the mountain showed a plain list of keys left from the Ascent's lab days (Backspace, Enter, N, Q). Now it opens the game's own menu, drawn like the road's: **Continue the climb**, **Nyxara** (her growth window), **The mountain** (seen from outside), **Settings** (the same settings window as on the start screen and the road), **Back to the last nest**, **A new climb** (forgets the save), **To the start screen** and **Leave the game**. W / S or the arrows or the mouse choose; Enter, Space or a click take; Esc goes back (or closes the settings window first). Nothing moves while it is open. The nature test (`CAUCASIAN_EAGLE_DEMO=5`) now ends by opening the menu and its settings window and fails if either does not open; pictured with `tools/release/Save-GameWindow.ps1`, which takes the game's own window and nothing else on the screen.

**Why Windows warns, and what 0.5.1 changes (2026-11-20).** The owner: "examine why windows shows our app is not safe". The audit is in [`docs/release-audits/v0.5-to-v0.5.1/REPORT.md`](release-audits/v0.5-to-v0.5.1/REPORT.md). The public 0.5.0 installer and zip are the files built from the `v0.5.0` commit, byte for byte; Microsoft Defender finds nothing in them. Nothing of ours is signed (the installer, `CaucasianEagle.exe`, the uninstaller), so Windows can name no publisher, and SmartScreen and Smart App Control have no reputation for the files: that is the warning. Signing needs a code-signing identity that only the owner can get (a certificate, or Microsoft Artifact Signing); everything else for it is ready: `tools/package.ps1 -Sign` signs the game before it is zipped and the installer and its uninstaller as Inno Setup builds them (`tools/release/Sign-Files.ps1`, SHA-256 with an RFC 3161 timestamp; how to sign is set in the git-ignored `tools/release/signing.local.json`, from `signing.template.json`; no secret is ever in the repository or on the command line). `tools/release/Test-Release.ps1` is the release gate: versions, checksums, GitHub's 2 GiB limit, that no other program ships, signatures (it fails an unsigned release unless told `-AllowUnsigned`), a Defender scan (`-Scan`), and with `-Published` that every public download link answers and gives the bytes its checksum names. The installer's own file properties now name the product and its copyright (0.5.0's had no copyright). For about an hour on 2026-11-20 the repository was private and every download link answered 404 to visitors; public again, all four 0.5.0 links answer and their checksums match. 0.5.0's files are left as they are.

**Version 0.5.1.** `GameConstants.h`, `CaucasianEagle.rc` and the installer script. Released unsigned, as the owner decided: signing comes with 1.0.0.

On the road this never happened; now that packs and flyers draw her out over the land and back it did: with the lift switched off the test pilot fell five times in 200 s in the Rokapi's clearing alone. The lift shows as a dip of less than a metre. The rim itself is in the world's collision (`art/world/build_world.py`, `build_land_collision`) and is not repaired: see debt in the tech plan.

**No safe corner in a fight.** The creatures of an ambush follow her as far as the walls. Before, they could not leave the clearing, and the stretch of road between the clearing and a wall was a safe place to shoot from. Guardians still keep to their clearings.

**Guide.** The journey page tells of traps and places; the fighting page tells what creatures do when she is in the air.

**She soars.** Travelling forward, Nyxara no longer beats her wings upright: she lies along her flight like an eagle, wings held wide and level with one slow stroke every three seconds, arms and swords swept back, 0.75 m over the ground, rising and settling slowly and banking into turns. Standing, backing, sidestepping and fighting she is upright, with slower, fuller wing beats than before. Her wings leave her back 10 cm lower, at the shoulder blades. (`art/nyxara/blender/assemble.py`: `ROOT_L`, `TRAVEL`, `soar_keys`; the hero bundle is recompiled, rig signature `DoorwaysRig_v1_116d1b0e1935a8eb`.)

**Left is left.** The engine shows the model as the mirror of what Blender shows (glTF is right-handed, the engine is not, nothing turns it over), so the bow made for her left hand was in her right on the screen. The bow is now bound to the rig's other hand and its pose is turned over: on the screen she holds it in her left hand and draws with her right. The axes' names follow the screen too: Inferno is in the hand you see as her right, and the first blow of a chain is RIGHT TALON.

**The bow, second pass.** Her hands: the `BowShot` pose is now built from where her chin and her bow shoulder really are (the first one used fixed places, and her string hand ended beside her face), on her Idle stance with her hips over the root: side-on, the bow upright in her left hand, her right hand at her chin, flying back as it lets go and coming round to take the string again; her wings are lowered and swept back so her arms show from behind. The bow's string is a material of its own (`Slot_bow_string`), hidden while she shoots: the game draws it bent to her hand, with an arrow of light on it, from the points `assemble.py` writes to `game/src/BowPoints.h`. Arrows do three times the damage (18, aimed 27). Aiming: as she raises the bow the view turns to the creature nearest its middle (22 degrees) or comes up level, and goes back when she lets go; the view narrows by a fifth; the mouse is slower still with the mark on a creature; the mark is red on a creature, four corners show the creature the arrows will go to, and ticks flash on a hit (gold on a kill); an aimed arrow is faster (110 m/s), far creatures are easier to be on, and an arrow bends a little to a creature that moves.

**A bow.** Nyxara carries a longbow (a Meshy model, Eldritch Longbow, `art/nyxara/meshy/out/bow_3d_1`) across her back. Q takes it out and puts the two axes on her back, crossed; Q again changes back. The axes' exchange of hands moved from Q to G. With the bow out, the right mouse button held aims: the camera comes in over her right shoulder (4.5 m, a new engine option `CameraSideOffset`) and a mark shows at the middle of the screen; the left button held looses arrows, five a second. An aimed arrow flies to the mark (9 damage); one loosed without aiming finds the nearest creature within 32 m ahead (6 damage). F, V and C take the axes out again. One new clip, `BowShot` (posed in `assemble.py`: her chest turned, the bow arm out, the string hand at her chin). Testing: `CAUCASIAN_EAGLE_BOW=1` (aims and looses) or `2` (looses without aiming) in an unattended run, with `[BOW]` log lines.

**Two axes.** Nyxara carries an axe in each hand: Inferno (a Meshy model, Molten Inferno Axe: black stone veined with lava) and Serpentcoil (Serpentcoil War Axe: dark iron), `art/nyxara/meshy/out/axe_inferno_3d_1` and `axe_war_3d_1`, each reduced to 7 000 triangles and about a metre long (`make_axe` in `art/nyxara/blender/assemble.py`). Her window (I) has an AXES row (Inferno and Serpentcoil, then Tempered, Moonforged and Eagle-forged axes; costs and the 20 % per evolution unchanged), runes are set in her axes, and the HUD names the axe in each hand. Tried and dropped on the way: one great scythe (two-handed, then held like a mower's, then trailing in one hand), and a time with no walking at all.

**Wilder.** She moves more like an eagle. Running, her back comes low (`WILD_LEAN` 30 degrees over her spine), her head stays up, and her wings are half open and beat with her stride; walking slowly she stalks the same way, less. Standing she is never still: over five seconds she looks left, then right, in the quick turns of a bird's head, cocks it, and shakes her wings out (`restless` in `assemble.py`). In flight she banks twice as hard into her turns (`SoarBank` 0.12).

**Space climbs in flight.** In the air, hold Space and she rises straight up, as far as the ceiling; X held sinks. W still flies where the camera looks. On foot Space is still the jump, and only R takes her up or lands her.

**Walking and flying are two ways of going, and only R changes them.** Walking: Nyxara walks (2.2 m/s), runs with Shift (4.6 m/s) and jumps with Space. R takes her up: she rises 6 m in 3 seconds and is flying. R again, within 6 m of what is under her, lands her: the change takes 3 seconds whatever her height, and she walks. Nothing else ends a flight: she fights flying, she may touch the ground flying (look up and press W to lift again), and opening a window does not drop her. Space does nothing while she flies. She no longer soars low over the ground by herself. A controller has no button for R yet.

**Roofs.** She can land on the buildings of the cities and walk on them: roofs, walls, towers, piers. The game reads each building's real shape (the highest surface of its mesh on a 96 x 96 map), so a pitched roof is a slope under her feet. From above a building carries her; from the ground its walls stop her as before. (Engine: `DoorwaysGameSession::SupportY`.)

**Alive in the air, and legs that match her pace.** In flight she no longer hangs like a statue: her wing strokes are deeper (hover 36 degrees; soaring 24 degrees every 2.4 s, was 13 every 3 s), her body rides each stroke about twice as far, and the game adds a slow rise and fall (11 cm) and a rocking of the body on rhythms of its own. Low in flight she hovers 0.8 m over the ground with her flight clips instead of standing. On foot she now goes at an easy jog of 3.2 m/s (2.2 before, when her legs ran faster than she moved), runs at 5.4 m/s with Shift and walks at 1.4 m/s with Ctrl; each uses the clip made for about that pace.

**Free flight goes where you look.** In the air W flies where the camera looks, so the mouse aims her up, down and round; with no key she hovers for as long as you like. No key climbs or sinks her. She can climb 45 m above where she took off. (Engine: `FreeFlight` with `FreeFlightSteer`, `FreeFlightTakeoff`, `FreeFlightTakeoffSeconds` and `FreeFlightKeyClimb = false`.)

**No moon.** The moon and its halo are gone from the sky of the game, and the engine no longer has a moon at all (its sphere, glow box, textures and settings are removed). The night, the stars and the moonlight on the land are as before. The start picture and the game's icon still show the Eagle before the moon: they are pictures, made outside the game.

**Talon Dash goes where you steer.** F dashed where the camera looked, snapped to a creature in front. It now goes the way the held W A S D keys point (against the camera, as travel does), or the way she is moving with the left stick, or the way she faces when nothing is held.

**Fighting sounds are hard and angry.** Blade swings tear the air and ring; a hit is a crack, a clang and a low punch; a kill adds a breaking blow (`combat/kill`) and Nyxara cries out in fury (`nyxara/rage`, at most once every 2.5 s). **No healing by itself.** The 1 health every 2 seconds is removed. Health comes from felled creatures, beaten waves, won arenas, cities and potions.

**A farther, higher, slower camera.** The camera follows from 15 m (was 7.4) and starts 29 degrees above her (was 19); the mouse wheel can pull it back to 18 m. It turns at 0.6 of the engine's speed (was 0.7). `CameraDistance`, `CameraMaxDistance`, `CameraPitch`, `CameraLookScale` in `game/src/GameConstants.h`.
