# Caucasian Eagle: story

[← Project index](../README.md)

This is the story bible for the game. Everything the game shows or says should agree with it.

**Status labels**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[author]** | Given by the game's author. Fixed unless the author changes it. |
| **[legend]** | Taken from the Georgian legend of Amirani. |
| **[proposed]** | Drafted to fill a gap. Not agreed yet; the author may replace it. |

---

## 1. The legend the game stands on

- **[legend]** Amirani fought against God. For that he is chained in a cave on the Caucasus Range.
- **[legend]** An eagle feeds on his liver without end.
- **[legend]** His faithful dog licks the chain to wear it thin and tries to break it.
- **[legend]** Every year, on the Thursday of Passion week (in the Tusheti telling, on the night before Christmas), blacksmiths appointed by God renew the chain, so the dog's work is undone.
- **[legend]** Once every seven years the cave opens and Amirani can be seen.

## 2. Premise

- **[author]** The eagle that eats Amirani's liver has a daughter, **Nyxara**.
- **[author]** On the day the seven years are up and the cave stands open, Nyxara sees Amirani by accident. She sees her own father eating his liver and is deeply confused.
- **[author]** She speaks with Amirani. In that conversation he wins her over with his charm, and asks her to train for the next seven years until she is strong enough to fight her father and open the chain that holds him to the mountain.
- **[author]** The whole game is her road to that mountain: hard ways, long fights and uncertain moments. At the end she fights her father to free Amirani.
- **[author]** Amirani chained on the mountain, with the eagle at his liver, is to be visible through the entire game.

### What this means for the game

| Story fact | Game consequence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| The mountain is always in view | Every map keeps the chained peak on the horizon; it grows nearer chapter by chapter | **[author]** intent, **[proposed]** execution |
| Seven years of training | The game's chapters are her seven years; each one teaches or tests one part of her fighting | **[proposed]** |
| She is the eagle's daughter | Wings, flight, talon-named skills, eagle helm; her enemy at the end fights with a stronger form of her own moves | **[proposed]** |
| Amirani "seduced the girl with his charm" | Whether Amirani is telling her the truth is the game's open question until the end | **[proposed]** |
| The chain is renewed every year | A yearly beat inside the game: the smiths' hammers are heard, and something she weakened grows strong again | **[proposed]** |

## 3. Characters

| Name | Who | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Nyxara** | Daughter of the eagle. Winged, two swords (Moonfeather and Talonshade), beaked eagle helm, silver, black and dark green. Playable hero. | **[author]** |
| **Amirani** | The chained hero. Charming, persuasive, and suffering. He asks Nyxara to free him. | **[author]** / **[legend]** |
| **The Eagle** | Nyxara's father. Set by God to feed on Amirani's liver. Final opponent. | **[author]** / **[legend]** |
| **The Dog** | Amirani's faithful dog, licking the chain thin every year. In the legend it is named Q'ursha. A possible guide or ally. | **[legend]**, role **[proposed]** |
| **The Smiths** | God-appointed blacksmiths who renew the chain once a year. Possible recurring opponents. | **[legend]**, role **[proposed]** |
| **Shades** | The dark wraiths Nyxara fights in v0.1. Story meaning not decided. | **[proposed]** |

## 4. Structure

**[proposed]** A prologue, seven chapters (one per year of training) and a finale.

| # | Part | What happens | Place |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **The Open Cave** | The seventh year ends, the cave opens. Nyxara sees Amirani and her father. The conversation. She agrees to train. | The mountain, seen close for the only time before the finale |
| 1 | **Year One: Moonlit Sanctuary** | She learns her swords and wings against shades. | Moonlit Sanctuary (the v0.1 map) |
| 2 to 6 | **Years Two to Six** | Harder roads toward the range. Each year ends with the sound of the smiths' hammers. | To be designed |
| 7 | **Year Seven: The Ascent** | The climb to the cave as the seven years close. | The mountain |
| 8 | **Father and Daughter** | The fight with the Eagle. | The cave mouth |
| 9 | **The Chain** | She opens the chain, or does not. | The cave |

## 5. Open story questions for the author

1. Is Amirani honest with Nyxara, or is he using her? Does the player find out?
2. Does Nyxara kill her father, defeat him without killing him, or is there a choice?
3. What happens when the chain opens? Is freeing Amirani good for the world?
4. What are the shades, and who sends them against her?
5. Do the Dog and the Smiths appear as characters, and on whose side?
6. How much is spoken aloud (dialogue, narration) and in which languages (English, Georgian, both)?
7. Where exactly on the Caucasus Range is the mountain, and should real place names be used?

## 6. What v0.1 contains of the story

Nothing is told in the game yet. Version 0.1 is the playable foundation: Nyxara, her combat, one map (Moonlit Sanctuary) and shades to fight. Story delivery starts with milestone M3 ([milestones](../milestones/README.md)).
