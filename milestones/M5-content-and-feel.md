# M5 · Content and feel (v0.5 to v0.9)

**Who:** You + Claude · **Needs:** M3 done (story spine), story structure agreed · **Next:** [M6](M6-release.md)

## Goal
The whole road is playable from the Open Cave to the chain, and it feels like a finished game.

This milestone is several versions. Each version adds playable chapters and one layer of polish; the split below is a proposal and will be re-planned after M3.

## Steps

### v0.5 · Years Two and Three
- [ ] Two new maps on the road toward the range; the mountain nearer in each.
- [ ] Enemy types that teach guard, aerial attacks and crowd control.
- [ ] The yearly "smiths' hammers" beat between chapters.

### v0.6 · Sound
- [ ] Music for menu, exploration, combat and the mountain (debt D10).
- [ ] Sound for slashes, skills, hits, wings, shades, UI.

### v0.7 · Years Four to Six
- [ ] Three more chapters; a mid-game opponent tied to the Smiths or the Dog, depending on the story answers.
- [ ] New skills or upgrades earned through the years.

### v0.8 · The game's own screens
- [ ] Main menu, pause, settings and death screens in the game's look (debt D8) **(engine: menu customisation)**.
- [ ] Key rebinding that includes the skill keys (debt D5).
- [ ] Decide the fate of the engine's level, experience and inventory systems (question P5).

### v0.9 · The mountain
- [ ] Year Seven: the ascent.
- [ ] The Eagle: the father-and-daughter fight.
- [ ] The chain: the ending or endings.
- [ ] Real aerial movement where the fight needs it (debt D7) **(engine)**.
- [ ] Performance pass and minimum requirements.

## ✅ Done when
- [ ] A player can go from the prologue to the ending without leaving the game.
- [ ] Every chapter has sound and music.
- [ ] Nothing on screen says "Doorways".
